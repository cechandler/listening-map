Keyboard shortcuts

Listening
SpacePlay or pause
Ctrl SpacePlay from the start of the selected block or entry
← →Skip 10 seconds; hold Shift for 1 second
CShow or hide the big clock
FFade out over 5 seconds and stop, then put the volume back; press again to cancel
Form and markers
SSplit the active lane at the playhead: the section lane or track you selected last
GGroup selected blocks into one block on the section lane above
JJoin the selected neighboring blocks (or track entries) into one
Shift + clickSelect more than one block or entry
DeleteJoin the selected block with its left neighbor (or delete a marker or track entry)
BackspaceJoin the selected block with its right neighbor
MAdd a marker at the playhead
Alt + arrowsMove the selection between blocks and section lanes; add Shift to extend
Tracks, while listening
1 to 9Make that track the active lane (numbered beside each name)
EThe active track enters at the playhead
XThe active track exits at the playhead
Editing and view
Ctrl ZUndo (also from a text box once its typing is undone)
Ctrl Shift Z or Ctrl YRedo
+ − 0Zoom in, zoom out, fit
Pinch, or Ctrl + scrollZoom the timeline around the pointer (trackpad, mouse wheel, or touchscreen)
Shift + scrollMove along the timeline with a mouse wheel
EscClear the selection
DragDrag a boundary or marker to adjust it. Drag across a track lane to add an entry. Hold Alt to ignore snapping.
