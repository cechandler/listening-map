Keyboard shortcuts

SpacePlay or pause
Ctrl SpacePlay from the start of the selected block
SSplit the active level at the playhead
GGroup selected blocks into one block on the level above
DeleteJoin the selected block with its left neighbor (or delete a marker or instrument entry)
BackspaceJoin the selected block with its right neighbor
MAdd a marker at the playhead
← →Skip 10 seconds; hold Shift for 1 second
Alt + arrowsMove the selection between blocks and levels; add Shift to extend
+ − 0Zoom in, zoom out, fit
Pinch, or Ctrl + scrollZoom the timeline around the pointer (trackpad, mouse wheel, or touchscreen; ⌘ on Mac)
Shift + scrollMove along the timeline with a mouse wheel
Ctrl Z / Ctrl YUndo and redo (⌘ on Mac)
EscClear the selection
DragDrag a boundary or marker to adjust it. Drag across an instrument lane to add an entry. Hold Alt to ignore snapping.
